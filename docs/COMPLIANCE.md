# Compliance Documentation

*Disclaimer: This document is for informational purposes and does not constitute legal advice.*

## US CAN-SPAM Act Checklist
For B2B emails sent to the US:
- [ ] **Accurate Header Info:** "From", "To", and "Reply-To" must accurately identify the sender.
- [ ] **Deceptive Subject Lines:** Prohibited. The subject must reflect the content.
- [ ] **Physical Address:** You must include a valid physical postal address in every email.
- [ ] **Opt-Out Mechanism:** Must include a clear and conspicuous way to opt out.
- [ ] **Opt-Out Processing:** Must honor opt-outs within 10 business days.

## UK PECR / EU GDPR Considerations
For B2B emails in the UK/EU:
- **Corporate Subscribers:** Generally, B2B emails to corporate addresses (e.g., info@company.com or john@company.com) are allowed under "Legitimate Interest" without prior consent, provided you offer an opt-out.
- **Sole Traders/Partnerships:** Often treated as individuals; prior consent is required.
- **Data Processing:** You must ensure lawful basis for processing personal data (the prospect's email and name).
- **Privacy Notice:** Provide a link to your privacy policy.

## LeadPilot Compliance Features

1. **Suppression Lists:** LeadPilot automatically prevents emailing contacts who have opted out or bounced.
2. **Required Footer:** Configure a required footer containing your physical address and unsubscribe link in the Settings -> Compliance tab.
3. **Data Retention:** Configure automatic deletion of prospect data after a certain period of inactivity.

## Record Keeping
Maintain logs of consent (if applicable) and opt-out requests. LeadPilot maintains an Audit Log for all compliance-related actions.
